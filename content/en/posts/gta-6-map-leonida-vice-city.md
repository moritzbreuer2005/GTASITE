GTA 6 takes place in the **state of Leonida**, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. Its biggest city is **Vice City**, Rockstar's take on Miami, which series fans first visited in 2002's GTA: Vice City.

## The regions of Leonida

Rockstar's official GTA 6 site introduced these areas around the second trailer:

- **Vice City:** The neon-lit main city, with beaches, nightlife and high-rise towers.
- **Leonida Keys:** A chain of islands in the south, based on the Florida Keys.
- **Grassrivers:** Swampy wetlands full of wildlife, inspired by the Everglades.
- **Port Gellhorn:** A rundown port town on the coast.
- **Ambrosia:** An industrial area in the countryside.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park:** Forests, hills and outdoor wilderness in the north.

## How big is the map?

Rockstar has not published an official size for the map. Fans have built detailed estimates from the trailers, and most expect it to be larger than GTA V's Los Santos and Blaine County. We will update this page with real numbers once players start exploring after launch.

## Why Leonida matters

Earlier GTA games focused on one city with some countryside around it. Leonida is a whole state, so the game mixes city life, small towns, swamps, beaches and islands. The trailers lean heavily on Florida culture: viral social media moments, wild nightlife and strange local news.
