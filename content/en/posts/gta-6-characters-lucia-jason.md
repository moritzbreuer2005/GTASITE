GTA 6 has two playable protagonists: **Lucia Caminos** and **Jason Duval**. Their partnership is at the center of the story, and the trailers frame them as a modern criminal couple in the style of Bonnie and Clyde.

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia is the first female lead in a mainline 3D Grand Theft Auto game. The first trailer opens with her in a prison jumpsuit. Rockstar's character profile describes her as someone who learned to fight early in life and wants a better future after time behind bars.

## Jason Duval

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, according to Rockstar's profile, and has spent time working in the Leonida Keys. He wants an easier life, but trouble keeps finding him. Teaming up with Lucia may be the riskiest decision he makes.

## Other characters revealed so far

Rockstar's official site also introduced several supporting characters before launch, including:

- **Cal Hampton:** Jason's friend, who is deeply suspicious of the government.
- **Boobie Ike:** A local legend in Vice City with businesses across the city.
- **Dre'Quan Priest:** A music manager trying to break artists through.
- **Real Dimez:** A hip-hop duo tied to Dre'Quan's label.
- **Raul Bautista:** An experienced bank robber.
- **Brian Heder:** A veteran smuggler in the Leonida Keys.

## Can you switch between characters?

Rockstar has not explained exactly how switching works. GTA V let you swap between three protagonists at almost any time, so many fans expect something similar. We will update this page once players get their hands on the game.
