**Grand Theft Auto VI releases on Thursday, November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version at launch.

## Quick answer

- **Release date:** November 19, 2026
- **Platforms:** PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
- **Unlock time:** Digital copies are reported to unlock at midnight in your local time zone
- **Preload:** Digital preloading was reported to open on November 10, so you can download the game before launch
- **PC:** Not announced. See [Is GTA 6 coming to PC?](post:is-gta-6-coming-to-pc)

## How to be ready to play at midnight

1. Buy or pre-order the digital version on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.
2. Start the preload as early as possible. The download is huge, so leave plenty of time.
3. Make sure your console has enough free storage. Delete old games or add an SSD if you need space.
4. Leave your console in rest mode (PS5) or connected standby (Xbox) so any day-one patch downloads before you sit down.
5. At midnight local time, launch the game from your library.

Physical copies depend on your retailer. Some shops sell at midnight, but most hand copies over during normal opening hours on launch day.

## The full delay history

GTA 6 has been a long wait. Grand Theft Auto V came out in September 2013, so this launch arrives more than 13 years later.

- **December 2023:** The first trailer confirms the game for a 2025 release.
- **May 2025:** Rockstar delays the game to May 26, 2026.
- **May 2025:** The second trailer shows Lucia and Jason in more detail.
- **November 2025:** Rockstar delays the game again, to November 19, 2026, saying it needs more time for polish.
- **November 19, 2026:** Launch day.
