**No PC version of GTA 6 has been announced.** Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

## What Rockstar has said

Rockstar and its parent company Take-Two have confirmed only the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions. Neither has announced a PC release date. Reports from industry insiders have suggested different PC windows, but none of them are official. Treat any specific PC date as a rumor until Rockstar confirms it.

> If you see a site selling a "GTA 6 PC download" right now, it is a scam. There is no legitimate PC version yet.

## What history tells us

Rockstar has a pattern of releasing on consoles first and bringing its games to PC later:

- **GTA V** came out on PS3 and Xbox 360 in September 2013. The PC version followed in April 2015, about 19 months later.
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** came out on consoles in October 2018. The PC version followed in November 2019, about 13 months later.

Based on that track record, a PC version of GTA 6 a year or more after the console launch would not be surprising. That is an educated guess, not an announcement.
