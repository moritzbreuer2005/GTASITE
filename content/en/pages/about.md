This is an independent fan site covering **Grand Theft Auto VI**: launch news, platform updates and guides to the game's world.

## What you will find here

- **Live news:** A headline feed from publishers around the web, refreshed automatically. Every headline links to the original article.
- **Guides:** Plain-language explainers that we keep updated as new information comes out.
