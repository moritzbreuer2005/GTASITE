This page explains what information this site collects and how it is used. The person responsible for this site is named in the [legal notice](page:imprint).

## Fonts

This site does not load fonts from outside servers. It only uses fonts already installed on your device.

## Information we collect

This site does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name or email address. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, for security and performance.

## Advertising and cookies

This site may use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may use cookies to credit this site with a commission.

## External links

Headlines on the news page link to other publishers' websites. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
